# Minutes

Write minutes from a recording transcript, notes, photos or a chat thread. Keep a link to the original.

From photos or screenshots, first write out what they show and have the user confirm names, dates and numbers, then write the minutes.

## Template

```markdown
# <Meeting title> · <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM, time zone>

Attendees: ...
Original: <link to the recording, transcript, thread or notes>

## Agenda
1. ...

## Discussion
### 1. <Topic>
- What was said, attributed when it matters
- Options considered

## Decisions
- DEC-007 <decision> (see the decision log)

## Action items
| What | Owner | Due |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Open questions
- ...
```

## Rules

- Separate what was decided from what was only discussed. A topic without a clear decision goes under open questions.
- Copy each decision into the decision log with the decider's words.
- Do not put private details that the meeting did not need, or any secret, into the minutes.
